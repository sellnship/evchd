Chandigarh's domestic electricity tariff is set by the **JERC five-year order (FY 2025-26 to 2029-30), effective 1 November 2025** — the first order after the CPDL privatisation. It is **tiered**: roughly **₹2.75/unit** at the lowest slab, around **₹4.80/unit** for 151–400 units, and about **₹5.40/unit** above 400 units, plus a small fixed charge and an electricity duty.

## What that means for a scooter

A low-speed electric scooter draws about **2 units** for a full charge. Rather than quote the cheapest slab, the honest basis for running-cost maths is an **all-in rate of about ₹5/unit** (mid-slab energy charge plus fixed charge and duty). On that basis:

- a full charge costs roughly **₹10** (less on the lowest slab);
- spread over a ~60 km range, that's about **₹0.17 per km**;
- the same distance on petrol costs in the region of **₹150–200**.

## Across the Tricity

Mohali (PSPCL) is around **₹5.40/unit** for the first 300 units, and Panchkula (Haryana, UHBVN) is broadly comparable. So the three cities are close on a typical bill — the ownable point is that Chandigarh's lower slabs reward modest domestic use, not that it is dramatically cheaper than its neighbours.

## What it means for a Tricity low-speed EV buyer

- A full overnight charge on a normal home socket costs about **₹10** at ~₹5/unit — no special connection needed.
- Budget on **~₹5/unit**, not the lowest slab, for an honest estimate; heavy household use climbs toward ₹5.40/unit.
- At **₹0.17/km**, running cost stays a small fraction of petrol wherever you plug in across the Tricity.
